      Featured Project

      Ultrasonic Milling for Legacy Iron

      Project Manager | September 2026–Present

      The engineering question

      Our team is evaluating ultrasonic milling against conventional ball milling for hematite ore. The comparison considers particle-size distribution, energy use, and process efficiency.

      My contribution

      • Lead a five-person team, coordinating project scope, responsibilities, client questions, and technical deliverables.
      • Develop an experimental plan around solids concentration, flow rate, and exposure time.
      • Plan particle-size comparisons using sieve analysis and mass measurements.
      • Organize test data and engineering comparisons in Microsoft Excel, including considerations around recirculation and energy use.

      Current status

      The project is ongoing. Our work focuses on developing the experimental plan and organizing the measurements needed to support a meaningful comparison.

      What I’m learning

      A useful process comparison depends on consistent test conditions and clear documentation. This project is helping me connect engineering decisions with practical constraints and communicate what the available evidence supports.
